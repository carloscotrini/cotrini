Projects & Open Resources

Software, tools, and open materials that came out of my research and teaching, free to read, run, and reuse.

Software & Tools

Browser extension · GDPR

CookieBlock

A browser extension that uses machine learning to enforce GDPR cookie consent on the client, classifying cookies by purpose so users can reject non-essential ones automatically. Built on our large-scale study of cookie-banner compliance (USENIX Security 2022, Distinguished Artifact Award).

Paper

Access control · research tool

Unicorn: a universal policy miner

A universal method for building access-control policy miners across many languages, including ABAC, RBAC, RBAC with spatio-temporal constraints, and a fragment of XACML, from only a language specification and a fitness metric. Introduced at ACM CCS 2019.

Paper

Open educational resource

Statistical Learning Theory: course script

An open, self-contained script on the theoretical foundations of machine learning, from PAC learning and VC dimension to Rademacher complexity and algorithmic stability.

GitHub · Course

Hands-on · reproducible

ML & DL Workshop (Dockerized)

Ready-to-run Docker images for a hands-on introduction to classification with machine and deep learning, requiring only basic Python, so anyone can reproduce the workshop locally.

From-Scratch Explainers

Tutorial · implementation

Transformers & Stable Diffusion, from scratch

A complete, minimal implementation of a transformer translator and a small stable-diffusion model, with intuition for every component. It is the basis of our SIGCSE 2026 award-winning study on full-stack machine-learning instruction.

Tutorial · Paper

Open lecture notes

Elementary explainers

Self-contained notes that build hard ideas from the ground up: an elementary introduction to quantum computing using only real linear algebra, the EM algorithm derived with a two-dimensional dog and a vegan flea, and algorithm validation via information theory.
